WHY A WEBSITE?

A gift made
of pixels & thought.

⌘

I'll be honest — I spent quite a bit of time wondering what to give you. I've never really made a birthday gift for someone like this before, and I didn't want to just pick something random because the date was coming up.

Then I thought: well, I know how to make websites. It's something I enjoy, and probably the most personal thing I could make with the skills I have right now. So I opened my editor and started building this little place.

It took more thought than I expected, and I'm sure there are tiny things I could keep tweaking forever. But that's the nice thing about making something yourself — it doesn't have to be perfect to mean something. I hope you like this little gift. ♡
